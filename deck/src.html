Problem statement

College gives you structure. YouTube doesn't.

  • No tests or feedback to show you've learned it
  • No plan or deadlines to keep you on track

Technical depth

We don't trust the model blindly

Closed topic list

Gemini may only cite topic IDs from the outline. Unknown IDs are dropped.

Checked timestamps

Validated against each video's real duration.

No invented sources

Questions can only cite moments we extracted.

Partial failure is fine

A failed, private or live video is skipped and listed.

Typed JSON

Pydantic validation on every response. Scores clamped to points.

Tested

Unit tests with a fake model and a fake YouTube API.

Scope

Built today, and what comes next

Built in this hackathon

  • Playlists up to 20 videos, outline as PDF or text
  • Course map with timestamp jumps
  • Suggested videos for missing topics
  • Questions per unit, graded answers
  • Study plan: weekly sessions with due dates from a start date and hours per week; unit tests and gap videos placed in those weeks; On track / weeks behind status
  • Study notes per session, built from what each video teaches, with timestamps, copyable as Markdown
  • Live on Cloud Run with Firestore

Next

  • Accounts and progress sync across devices
  • Outlines for common job roles
  • Hindi, regional languages, Android
Feasibility, impact & viability

Cheap to run, useful from day one

Feasibility

  • Gemini reads YouTube links and PDFs natively: no downloads, transcripts or embeddings
  • One container, one deploy command
  • About 160k tokens per video-hour at low resolution, cached for later students
  • Videos analysed 4 at a time; private or failed ones skipped

Impact

  • Students see what a playlist covers and skips, and get graded feedback
  • Colleges support skills beyond the syllabus and placement prep
  • Creators keep their views through the official embed
  • Measured by test scores on first vs. second attempt

Business model

  • Free for students
  • B2B: skill tracks and progress dashboards for college placement cells
  • Partners: coverage reports for creators against common skill outlines
