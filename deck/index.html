Problem statement

Playlists help, but they don't follow your syllabus

Lectures come first. Students use YouTube alongside them to revise, go deeper, and learn skills outside the course.

  • No way to tell what a playlist covers or skips
  • The topic you need is buried in hours of video
  • Watching a lecture isn't practising an answer

Technical depth

We don't trust the model blindly

Closed topic list

Gemini may only cite syllabus topic IDs. Unknown IDs are dropped.

Checked timestamps

Validated against each video's real duration.

No invented sources

Questions can only cite moments we extracted.

Partial failure is fine

A failed, private or live video is skipped and listed.

Typed JSON

Pydantic validation on every response. Scores clamped to marks.

Tested

Unit tests with a fake model and a fake YouTube API.

Feasibility & viability

Small to build, cheap to run

Why it fits in 6 hours

  • Gemini reads YouTube links and PDFs natively
  • No download, transcription or embeddings
  • One container, one deploy command

Running cost

  • About 160k input tokens per lecture-hour at low resolution
  • Cached, so later students cost close to nothing
